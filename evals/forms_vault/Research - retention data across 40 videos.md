# Research - retention data across 40 videos

Pulled from the analytics export on 2026-05-02, last 40 uploads.

- Videos opening with a concrete example: mean retention 43%.
- Videos opening with a definition or a thesis statement: mean retention 31%.
- The gap holds after controlling for video length.
- Sponsored segments before the 3 minute mark cost about 6 points of retention;
  after the 8 minute mark the cost is not measurable.

This is the evidence behind [[Hook patterns that actually retain]].
