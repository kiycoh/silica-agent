# ep-171 I built a zero-cost editing pipeline

Published 2026-04-18. Highest retention I have ever hit at 47%.

## The build

Whisper large-v3 running on the same 4090 I dismissed in [[ep-142 Why I stopped using local AI models]].
Transcription for a 40 minute video takes about 4 minutes and costs nothing.
Over a year that is roughly 2,900 EUR of Descript and API spend I no longer pay.

**Local transcription is the single highest-ROI thing in my pipeline.** It runs
overnight, it never rate-limits me, and the client footage never leaves the studio,
which is what let me sign the [[Sponsor - Kestrel Audio contract terms]] deal at all.

## Claims I made on camera

- Local Whisper is more accurate than the paid transcription services on accented English.
- The pipeline paid back the GPU in five months.
