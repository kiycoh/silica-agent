# Research - local inference cost math

Numbers for [[Ideas - the local-first creator studio]].

- RTX 4090, bought 2025-08, 1,750 EUR.
- Studio power: 0.28 EUR/kWh. The box pulls ~420W under transcription load.
- 2025 spend on cloud transcription and captions: 2,880 EUR across the year.
- Whisper large-v3 on 40 minutes of 1080p audio: 3m52s wall clock, measured 2026-02-11.

Payback: 5.1 months on transcription alone, before counting upscaling.

Caveat I keep forgetting: this ignores the six weeks I burned on setup in 2025,
which is exactly the cost I complained about in [[ep-142 Why I stopped using local AI models]].
