# Hook patterns that actually retain

The pattern library, rebuilt from [[Research - retention data across 40 videos]].

- **Reversal**: promise the expected video, deliver its opposite. Best case is
  [[ep-142 Why I stopped using local AI models]].
- **Concrete first**: open on one specific object or number, never a definition.
  Violating this is what sank [[ep-158 The benchmark theater problem]].
- **Cost stated up front**: "this cost me 1,750 EUR" outperforms "this is expensive".
